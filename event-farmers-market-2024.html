Rise & Rally: Pickleball for a Purpose

Friendly competition, sweet treats, and a cause we care about deeply

Our first-ever pickleball fundraiser brought together athletes, friends, and supporters for a day of competition, homemade treats, and community impact.

Through every match played and every item sold at our bake sale, we raised funds to support women facing breast cancer and partered with a local organization Pink Promises that also strives to support low-income women undergoing breast cancer treatment.

Thank you everyone who came out to support us and made this event a success and congratulations to the winners of the tournament, Tim and Parrie!
